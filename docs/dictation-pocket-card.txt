EMS DICTATION POCKET CARD
Talk through these 12 in any order. Fragments are fine.
Skip what's on the monitor upload or in structured fields.

1. CALL FRAME
   Unit / dispatch complaint / response mode
   Scene type / other agencies + their role
   Delays and why

2. ARRIVAL PICTURE
   Where found, position, first impression
   Who was present
   Scene observations that shaped decisions

3. PATIENT
   Age / sex / est. weight
   Baseline status if known

4. STORY
   Chief complaint in their words
   Onset / duration / mechanism
   Better-worse / before EMS arrived
   Who gave history + reliability

5. PERTINENT NEGATIVES
   What the patient specifically DENIED

6. EXAM HIGHLIGHTS
   Only findings that drove decisions
   or aren't in the Assessment tab

7. NUMBERS
   Vitals not on upload / trends
   Anything abnormal + your read on why

8. THINKING
   Working diagnosis
   What else you considered + why less likely
   Protocol used (name or number)

9. DOING
   Each treatment + why
   Anything withheld + why
   Patient response

10. MOVING
    Transport decision + destination rationale
    How moved / position + why
    Condition on arrival

11. HANDOFF
    Report given to whom
    Records, lines, devices, belongings

12. EXCEPTIONS
    Anything unusual / refused interventions
    Delays / equipment issues
    What a reviewer should understand

questions instead of a blank interview.
