## Why CC BY

Documentation quality and AI governance in paramedicine are profession-wide problems,
not competitive advantages. The goal of this publication is adoption, adaptation, and
improvement by the broadest possible community of providers, agencies, educators,
researchers, and international paramedicine systems -- including those who build on
this work commercially.

The only requirement is credit. If this work is useful to you, say where it came from.
